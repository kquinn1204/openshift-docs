= Standards compliance for Telecom Grandmaster on Intel Granite Rapids-D

[role="_abstract"]
Telecom Grandmaster (T-GM) deployments on Intel Granite Rapids-D (GNR-D) platforms comply with specific ITU-T timing standards for accuracy, holdover, and traceability.
Understand the supported standards, known non-conformances, and general availability (GA) conformance requirements before deploying T-GM in production environments.

== Standards compliance overview

Telecom Grandmaster deployments on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware comply with specific ITU-T timing standards with known non-conformances documented below.

.ITU-T standards compliance and conformance status
[cols="1,2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Standard
|Requirement
|Specification
|Notes

4+^|*ITU-T G.8272 PRTC-B compliance*

|G.8272
|Timing accuracy
|±40 nanoseconds relative to UTC
|When locked to GNSS time source, meets PRTC-B classification requirements.

|G.8272
|Traceability
|GNSS antenna connected to NAC adapter
|Maintains traceability to UTC through continuous GNSS signal reception and DPLL synchronization.

|G.8272
|Classification
|PRTC-B
|Operates as PRTC-B clock when locked to GNSS, providing highest timing accuracy classification for telecom applications.

4+^|*ITU-T G.8273.2 Class-B holdover compliance*

|G.8273.2
|Holdover duration
|Minimum 4 hours
|Microchip DPLL maintains timing accuracy during GNSS signal loss.

|G.8273.2
|Time error during holdover
|Maximum ±1.5 microseconds
|Ensures downstream devices continue receiving timing within Class-B specifications during holdover period.

|G.8273.2
|Holdover validation
|Disconnect GNSS antenna and monitor timing offset
|For more information, see "Verifying Telecom Grandmaster DPLL synchronization on Intel Granite Rapids-D hardware".

4+^|*ITU-T G.8275.1 profile conformance status*

|G.8275.1
|Conformance status
|Partial compliance
|Implements PTP profile but does not claim full conformance due to known non-conformances and deferred SyncE support.

|G.8275.1
|Known non-conformances
|Documented with PM approval
|Validation testing with Paragon-neo identified specific test cases that do not pass. All failures are formally documented.

|G.8275.1
|SyncE support
|Deferred to future release
|Full ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance requires SyncE support, which is not included in {product-title} {product-version}.

4+^|*Paragon-neo validation test results*

|Paragon-neo
|Test cases passed
|Majority
|T-GM passes the majority of Paragon-neo test cases for ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance.

|Paragon-neo
|Test cases failed
|Specific cases with waivers
|Product management reviews each non-conformance and approves waivers for known limitations that do not impact telco deployment requirements.

|Paragon-neo
|Non-conformance documentation
|All failures documented
|Each failure includes detailed description of failure mode, impact analysis, and product management approval.

4+^|*General availability conformance criteria*

|GA criteria
|Paragon-neo documentation
|All failures documented
|Every Paragon-neo test failure must be documented as a formal non-conformance with product management sign-off.

|GA criteria
|PM approval for waivers
|Required
|Product management must review and approve waivers for all known non-conformances.

|GA criteria
|SyncE general availability
|Required
|SyncE support must reach general availability to claim full ITU-T G.8275.1 conformance. SyncE GA is a prerequisite for T-GM GA status.
|===
